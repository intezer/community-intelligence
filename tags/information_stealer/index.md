## Posts

- [YTStealer Malware: "YouTube Cookies! Om Nom Nom Nom"](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2022/06/ytstealer-malware-youtube-cookies/) (2022-06-29): YTStealer steals only YouTube authentication cookies, using anti-analysis code from the Chacal project. Its build path names YouBot Solutions.
- [New Conversation Hijacking Campaign Delivering IcedID](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2022/03/conversation-hijacking-campaign-delivering-icedid/) (2022-03-28): An IcedID campaign hijacks email threads from compromised Exchange servers and delivers ISO files with LNK and DLL payloads to bypass Mark-of-the-Web.
- [Operation ElectroRAT: Attacker Creates Fake Companies to Drain Your Crypto Wallets](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2021/01/operation-electrorat-attacker-creates-fake-companies-to-drain-your-crypto-wallets/) (2021-01-05): ElectroRAT, a new Golang RAT for Windows, Linux and macOS, spreads through trojanized crypto apps promoted by fake companies to drain victims’ wallets.
