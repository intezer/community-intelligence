## Posts

- [Threat Bulletin: Weaponized Software Targets Chinese-Speaking Organizations](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2025/01/weaponized-software-targets-chinese/) (2025-01-16): Trojanized MSI installers aimed at Chinese-speaking organizations use a loader we named PNGPlug to deliver ValleyRAT, in a campaign linked to Silver Fox.
- [Technical Analysis of a Novel IMEEX Framework](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2024/10/technical-analysis-of-a-novel-imeex-framework/) (2024-10-10): IMEEX is a custom Windows DLL framework seen targeting Djibouti and Afghanistan, with remote control features and signs of infrastructure reuse with ShadowPad.
- [New Linux Backdoor RedXOR Likely Operated by Chinese Nation-State Actor](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2021/03/new-linux-backdoor-redxor-likely-operated-by-chinese-nation-state-actor/) (2021-03-10): RedXOR, a Linux backdoor posing as polkit daemon, shares traits with Winnti-linked PWNLNX and XOR.DDOS and is likely operated by a Chinese nation-state actor.
- [The Evolution of APT15's Codebase 2020](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2020/05/the-evolution-of-apt15s-codebase-2020/) (2020-05-21): Ketrum, a new APT15 backdoor family merging features of Ketrican and Okrum, shows the group reworking the same codebase rather than changing tactics.
- [ChinaZ Updates Toolkit by Introducing New, Undetected Malware](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2019/12/chinaz-updates-toolkit-by-introducing-new-undetected-malware/) (2019-12-13): ChinaZ is using new low-detection Linux malware, NewManager, AmpManager and DDoSManager, which combine backdoor and DDoS bot capabilities.
- [Evidence Aurora Operation Still Active Part 2: More Ties Uncovered Between CCleaner Hack & Chinese Hackers](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2017/10/evidence-aurora-operation-still-active-part-2-more-ties-uncovered-between-ccleaner-hack-chinese-hackers/) (2017-10-02): Code shared by the CCleaner stage 2 payload and APT17 samples strengthens the link to the Axiom group, in an attack we assess was most likely state-sponsored.
- [Evidence Aurora Operation Still Active: Supply Chain Attack Through CCleaner](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2017/09/evidence-aurora-operation-still-active-supply-chain-attack-through-ccleaner/) (2017-09-20): A unique base64 function shared by the CCleaner 5.33 backdoor and earlier APT17 samples suggests the Operation Aurora group was still active in 2017.
