## Posts

- [Wrapping Up a Year of Infamous Bazar Campaigns](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2021/05/wrapping-up-a-year-of-infamous-bazar-campaigns/) (2021-05-27): A profile of Bazar, the backdoor attributed to the TrickBot gang and named for its EmerDNS .bazar C2 domains, and four prominent campaigns from its first year.
- [Stantinko's Proxy After Your Apache Server](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2020/11/stantinkos-proxy-after-your-apache-server/) (2020-11-24): A new version of Stantinko’s Linux proxy trojan masquerades as Apache httpd. We analyze version 2.17 and compare it with version 1.2, documented by ESET.
- [ChinaZ Updates Toolkit by Introducing New, Undetected Malware](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2019/12/chinaz-updates-toolkit-by-introducing-new-undetected-malware/) (2019-12-13): ChinaZ is using new low-detection Linux malware, NewManager, AmpManager and DDoSManager, which combine backdoor and DDoS bot capabilities.
- [Mapping the Connections Inside Russia’s APT Ecosystem](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2019/09/russian-apt-ecosystem/) (2019-09-24): Joint research with Check Point mapping thousands of Russian APT samples finds actors rarely share code, which may point to a focus on operational security.
- [Technical Analysis: Pacha Group Competing against Rocke Group for Cryptocurrency Mining Foothold on the Cloud](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2019/05/technical-analysis-cryptocurrency-mining-war-on-the-cloud/) (2019-05-09): New Linux.GreedyAntd variants show Pacha Group targeting cloud environments and using blacklists to remove Rocke Group miners and cloud security agents.
