## Posts

- [Frankenstein Variant of the ToneShell Backdoor Targeting Myanmar](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2025/09/frankenstein-variant-of-the-toneshell-backdoor-targeting-myanmar/) (2025-09-10): A new variant of Mustang Panda’s ToneShell backdoor targeting Myanmar uses anti-analysis stalling, API hashing and updated GUID creation. Includes new IoCs.
- [Threat Bulletin: Fire in the Woods – A New Variant of FireWood](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2025/08/threat-bulletin-firewood/) (2025-08-13): A new, low-detected variant of FireWood, a Linux backdoor of the Project Wood lineage, keeps its core functions but changes implementation and configuration.
- [Threat Bulletin: Weaponized Software Targets Chinese-Speaking Organizations](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2025/01/weaponized-software-targets-chinese/) (2025-01-16): Trojanized MSI installers aimed at Chinese-speaking organizations use a loader we named PNGPlug to deliver ValleyRAT, in a campaign linked to Silver Fox.
- [Technical Analysis of a Novel IMEEX Framework](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2024/10/technical-analysis-of-a-novel-imeex-framework/) (2024-10-10): IMEEX is a custom Windows DLL framework seen targeting Djibouti and Afghanistan, with remote control features and signs of infrastructure reuse with ShadowPad.
- [Operation HamsaUpdate: A Sophisticated Campaign Delivering Wipers Puts Israeli Infrastructure at Risk](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2023/12/stealth-wiper-israeli-infrastructure/) (2023-12-20): Phishing aimed at Israeli F5 users abused a real BIG-IP flaw to deliver new wipers, Hatef for Windows and Hamsa for Linux, plus the Handala AutoIt loader.
- [WildCard: The APT Behind SysJoker Targets Critical Sectors in Israel](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2023/11/wildcard-evolution-of-sysjoker-cyber-threat/) (2023-11-27): WildCard, the APT behind SysJoker, targets critical sectors in Israel with new variants and the Rust-based RustDown, and has links to Operation ElectricPowder.
- [Phishing Campaign Targets Chinese Nuclear Energy Industry](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2023/03/phishing-campaign-targets-nuclear-energy-industry/) (2023-03-24): A phishing campaign against China’s nuclear energy industry delivers Excel and CHM payloads, using tactics and techniques that align with Bitter APT.
- [Elephant Framework Delivered in Phishing Attacks Against Ukrainian Organizations](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2022/04/elephant-malware-targeting-ukrainian-orgs/) (2022-04-04): Elephant, a Go malware framework that includes GrimPlant and GraphSteel, is delivered by spear phishing from spoofed Ukrainian government email addresses.
- [Targeted Phishing Attack against Ukrainian Government Expands to Georgia](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2021/07/targeted-phishing-attack-against-ukrainian-government-expands-to-georgia/) (2021-07-14): A spear phishing campaign against Ukraine’s government expanded to Georgia with an AutoIt infostealer. Similarities to APT28 suggest a possible Russian link.
- [New Linux Backdoor RedXOR Likely Operated by Chinese Nation-State Actor](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2021/03/new-linux-backdoor-redxor-likely-operated-by-chinese-nation-state-actor/) (2021-03-10): RedXOR, a Linux backdoor posing as polkit daemon, shares traits with Winnti-linked PWNLNX and XOR.DDOS and is likely operated by a Chinese nation-state actor.
- [A Zebra in Gopher’s Clothing: Russian APT Uses COVID-19 Lures to Deliver Zebrocy](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2020/12/russian-apt-uses-covid-19-lures-to-deliver-zebrocy/) (2020-12-09): Sofacy (APT28) used COVID-19 lures about Sinopharm, delivered in VHD files, to drop the Go version of Zebrocy, linked to Sofacy through shared code.
- [The Evolution of APT15's Codebase 2020](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2020/05/the-evolution-of-apt15s-codebase-2020/) (2020-05-21): Ketrum, a new APT15 backdoor family merging features of Ketrican and Okrum, shows the group reworking the same codebase rather than changing tactics.
- [New Iranian Campaign Tailored to US Companies Utilizes an Updated Toolset](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2020/01/new-iranian-campaign-tailored-to-us-companies-uses-updated-toolset/) (2020-01-30): APT34 targets Westat employees and the US firms that hire Westat with a malicious survey spreadsheet, dropping undetected TONEDEAF 2.0 and VALUEVAULT 2.0.
- [ChinaZ Updates Toolkit by Introducing New, Undetected Malware](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2019/12/chinaz-updates-toolkit-by-introducing-new-undetected-malware/) (2019-12-13): ChinaZ is using new low-detection Linux malware, NewManager, AmpManager and DDoSManager, which combine backdoor and DDoS bot capabilities.
- [Mapping the Connections Inside Russia’s APT Ecosystem](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2019/09/russian-apt-ecosystem/) (2019-09-24): Joint research with Check Point mapping thousands of Russian APT samples finds actors rarely share code, which may point to a focus on operational security.
- [EvilGnome: Rare Malware Spying on Linux Desktop Users](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2019/07/evilgnome-rare-malware-spying-on-linux-desktop-users/) (2019-07-17): EvilGnome is an undetected Linux backdoor posing as a Gnome shell extension that takes screenshots, records audio and steals files. Linked to Gamaredon Group.
- [Paleontology: The Unknown Origins of Lazarus Malware](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2018/10/paleontology-the-unknown-origins-of-lazarus-malware/) (2018-10-31): Lazarus malware appears to be built on CasperPhpTrojan, an open-source RAT from a Chinese code site, found via a low-detection sample we call CasperTroy.
- [APT37: Final1stspy Reaping the FreeMilk](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2018/10/apt37-final1stspy-reaping-the-freemilk/) (2018-10-03): An earlier Final1stspy DLL shares unique code with ROKRAT from the FreeMilk campaign, adding evidence that links NOKKI and Final1stspy to APT37 (Group123).
- [Prince of Persia: The Sands of Foudre](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2018/08/prince-of-persia-the-sands-of-foudre/) (2018-08-17): Foudre version 8 shows the Prince of Persia (Infy) campaign, believed to be Iranian, is still active, using a protest video as a lure and an updated DGA.
- [Examining Code Reuse Reveals Undiscovered Links Among North Korea’s Malware Families](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2018/08/examining-code-reuse-reveals-undiscovered-links-among-north-koreas-malware-families/) (2018-08-06): Joint research with McAfee maps code reuse across malware attributed to North Korea, from 2009 attacks to WannaCry, revealing links between groups and families.
- [Yet Another Distraction? A New Version of North Korean Ransomware Hermes Has Emerged](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2018/02/another-distraction-new-version-north-korean-ransomware-hermes/) (2018-02-08): Hermes 2.1 ransomware shares code with the Hermes version used as a diversion in a Taiwan bank heist and thought to originate from North Korea’s Lazarus group.
- [BLOCKBUSTED: Lazarus, Blockbuster, and North Korea](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2017/12/blockbusted-lazarus-blockbuster-north-korea/) (2017-12-12): Code overlaps in Lazarus samples from 2014 to 2017, including shared C&C command handlers, link Blockbuster campaign malware to other North Korean attacks.
- [NotPetya Returns as Bad Rabbit](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2017/10/notpetya-returns-bad-rabbit/) (2017-10-25): Bad Rabbit ransomware reuses NotPetya code: 27% of its fake Adobe Flash installer loader and 13% of its encryption module match NotPetya samples.
- [Evidence Aurora Operation Still Active Part 2: More Ties Uncovered Between CCleaner Hack & Chinese Hackers](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2017/10/evidence-aurora-operation-still-active-part-2-more-ties-uncovered-between-ccleaner-hack-chinese-hackers/) (2017-10-02): Code shared by the CCleaner stage 2 payload and APT17 samples strengthens the link to the Axiom group, in an attack we assess was most likely state-sponsored.
- [Evidence Aurora Operation Still Active: Supply Chain Attack Through CCleaner](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2017/09/evidence-aurora-operation-still-active-supply-chain-attack-through-ccleaner/) (2017-09-20): A unique base64 function shared by the CCleaner 5.33 backdoor and earlier APT17 samples suggests the Operation Aurora group was still active in 2017.
- [New Variants of Agent.BTZ/ComRAT Found: The Threat That Hit The Pentagon In 2008 Still Evolving; Part 2/2](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2017/09/new-variants-of-agent-btz-comrat-found-part-2/) (2017-09-13): How we hunted new Agent.BTZ/ComRAT variants: filtering out library code, isolating unique functions kept since 2007 and turning them into YARA rules.
- [New Variants of Agent.BTZ/ComRAT Found: The Threat That Hit The Pentagon In 2008 Still Evolving; Part 1/2](https://research.intezer.com/blog/2017/08/new-variants-of-agent-btz-comrat-found/) (2017-08-07): New Agent.BTZ/ComRAT variants from the Turla group: about a dozen new samples and over 70 unknown IPs and domains abusing satellite internet providers.
